# PAPER_READING Mode

## Goal
Explain what the paper claims, how the method works, what evidence supports it,
where the evidence is weak, and what the paper means for the audience's work.

## Default emphasis
Background: ~15–25%
Method/mechanism: ~30–40%
Experiments/analysis: ~30–40%
Limitations/discussion: ~10–20%

Method + experiments should normally exceed half of substantive slides.

## Workflow
1. identify research question and gap;
2. identify MAIN_METHOD_FIGURE;
3. identify MAIN_RESULT_FIGURE_OR_TABLE;
4. identify MAIN_ABLATION_OR_ANALYSIS;
5. preflight all Tier-A/B source visuals;
6. build narrative around claims and evidence;
7. only create custom diagrams when source visuals are insufficient and the
   Diagram Safety Gate passes.

## Strong paper-reading sequence
problem → observed limitation → insight → method overview → mechanism →
experimental question → main result → ablation → analysis → limitation →
connection to our work.

Do not follow paper section order mechanically.

## Contributions
Rewrite marketing language into concrete technical change.

## Main results
Explain metric, strongest baseline, ours, delta, uncertainty, exceptions, and
what the result actually establishes.

## Ablation
State the changed component and the mechanism the result is meant to diagnose.

## Discussion
Separate author claim, measured evidence, your interpretation, and open question.
