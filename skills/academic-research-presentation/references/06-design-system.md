# Scientific Design System

## Character
Restrained, technical, readable, academic.

Avoid:
startup pitch, SaaS dashboard, cinematic gradients, generic AI imagery.

## Typography
Typical:
- title: 24–30 pt
- body: 16–20 pt
- annotation: 14–17 pt
- citation: 9–11 pt

Use reliable fonts. Nontrivial mathematics should use proper equation rendering.

## Background
White / near-white default.
Institutional templates may be used, but page furniture must not crowd evidence.

## Color
Small, semantic palette.
Same semantic role = same color across slides.
Do not rely on color alone.

## Shapes
No rectangle around ordinary text.
No shadows/nested cards by default.
Boxes are for real components or semantic groups.

## Tables
Light rules, clear header, restrained highlighting.
Highlight only comparisons relevant to the spoken argument.

## Figures
Large, complete, legible, directly annotated when useful.

## Layout
Use a stable grid but allow asymmetry when the scientific evidence demands it.
Do not force unequal ideas into equal-width containers.
