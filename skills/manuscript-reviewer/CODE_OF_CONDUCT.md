# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented. Critique contributions and scientific-review behavior rather than contributors. Do not publish confidential manuscripts, private reviewer reports, credentials, or personal data in issues or pull requests. Harassment, discriminatory conduct, threats, and deliberate disclosure of private material are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or confidential research material.
