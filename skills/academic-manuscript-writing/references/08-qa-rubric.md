# Manuscript QA rubric

## Hard failures

Any of the following blocks release:

- fabricated or unsupported scientific claim;
- number inconsistent with authoritative evidence;
- wrong direction/comparator/population/timepoint;
- invented method detail;
- dangling or incorrect figure/table reference, including a source/display-number identity mismatch;
- causal claim unsupported by design;
- hidden material contradiction across sections;
- stale claim ledger text/section placement relative to its declared manuscript anchor;
- v1.8 governed claim span missing its end marker, drifting from ledger text, owning the wrong citation/object, overlapping another span, or leaving material scientific prose outside all governed spans;
- a main release claim whose declared method or material qualifier dependency is absent from the manuscript;
- a superseded/retired claim that still remains in the current manuscript;
- a completed reviewer/editor manuscript-change obligation that lacks a verified change/diff trail;
- unresolved placeholder presented as final text;
- unresolved **blocking** source conflict, or a `disclose_and_scope` conflict without current scoped/disclosure claim coverage;
- obvious compound result/method/limitation claim that hides multiple independent propositions without a justified atomicity exemption;
- revise/refresh manuscript that violates the declared preservation/locality policy or replaces scientific prose with workflow/audit-report narration;
- citation presented as real without a supplied/verified source.

## Review dimensions

### Scientific correctness
Does each sentence preserve the actual analysis and study design?

### Evidence completeness
Are primary, null, negative, and qualifying results represented proportionately?

### Claim calibration
Does wording match evidence strength?

### Numeric fidelity
Do estimate, uncertainty, p-value, n, units, and rounding agree everywhere?

### Scope fidelity
Are population, task, dataset, organism, and setting boundaries preserved?

### Figure/table fidelity
Do text and captions match the actual object and panel?

### Cross-section consistency
Do Abstract, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, captions, and supplement tell the same scientific story? Are inactive historical claims absent from the current manuscript?

### Limitations
Are interpretation-changing limitations visible and correctly attached to claims?

### Narrative logic
Does the manuscript progress by scientific argument rather than generic filler?

### Style
Is prose precise, compact, and readable without rhetorical inflation?

## Release rule

A polished style cannot compensate for a hard scientific failure.
